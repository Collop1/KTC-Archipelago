# Kingdom Two Crowns

## What is randomized?

- Things you find out in the world: chests (coin and gem), hermits, mounts, statues, portals to destroy
- Coin/gem chests and portal destructions are sent as progressive checks - the next unopened one in a fixed
  order fires the check, regardless of which physical chest/portal you interact with

## What items can be received?

- Progressive Castle Unlock tiers (T1-T7)
- Stone Technology and Iron Technology (Stone lets you destroy portals; Iron lets you liberate islands)
- "Use" items for hermits, mounts and statues (you can still build/find them without the item, but can't pay to
  use them until it's received)

## What is the goal?

Liberate all five islands from the Greed.

## Are DLCs supported?

Not yet.
